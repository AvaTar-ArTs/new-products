## Channel strategy

### Setapp

Use Setapp for one polished native app at a time. The strongest candidates are
WorkVault Control, Creator Asset Intelligence, and Archive Atlas because their
value is visual review, safe actions, and ongoing desktop utility. A CLI alone
is not a Setapp submission; it needs a native GUI, signed/notarized artifact,
permissions review, onboarding, and a clean-Mac test matrix.

### Product Hunt

Launch one sharp story per campaign: “I can finally stop a stuck Mac update
without losing the run,” “I found every real duplicate across three drives,”
or “I turned 19,000 scripts into a product backlog.” Show the evidence and
ask for feedback; do not launch the entire ecosystem as one listing.

### Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy

Sell bounded digital products first: Pro CLI binaries, report templates,
starter fixtures, workflow playbooks, onboarding, and update access. This is
the fastest route for Archive Atlas, Portfolio Forge, Creator Commerce
Foundry, and Agent Workspace Bridge while native apps mature.

### GitHub, PyPI, Homebrew

Use a free/community core as the trust layer. Keep paid value in review UI,
redacted exports, policies, restore manifests, support, and repeatable
consultant workflows—not in mysterious destructive automation.

## Commercial boundaries

1. No product ships the entire private workspace.
2. No external publishing runs without an approval gate.
3. No duplicate claim is based on filename alone; content identity and
   provenance are required.
4. No backup is deleted by a default command.
5. No current-market demand or revenue outcome is claimed from local inventory
   alone; those are hypotheses until validated with buyers.

## Recommended build order

1. WorkVault Control: public CLI and first demo.
2. Archive Atlas: strongest cross-volume differentiator and consultant offer.
3. Python Portfolio Forge: use the existing 19,244-file evidence as a
   sanitized case study.
4. Creator Commerce Foundry: package existing Etsy/Suno/SEO capabilities into
   export-only kits.
5. Native Setapp candidate: choose the product with repeat usage, not the one
   with the biggest feature list.
